Data Sources

Get Britain Working: Labour Market Insights

Published by DWP quarterly, starting October 2025. The January 2026 release provides:

  • UC 'Searching for work' into-work rates by geography, duration, age, and family type
  • 14 local labour market types based on cluster analysis
  • Employment sector distributions for UC recipients
  • Health journey and work capability data

Access the full release

NEET Statistics Annual Brief

Published by DfE, provides 16-17 NEET estimates by local authority. Key caveats:

  • NCCIS figures include 'not known' activity, which may overestimate NEET
  • 'Not known' proportions vary by local authority, complicating comparisons
  • Different methodologies apply to 16-17 (admin data) vs 18-24 (survey data)
ONS Young People NEET Bulletin

Quarterly bulletin for 16-24 NEET based on Labour Force Survey. Notes:

  • LFS data quality issues in 2023 led to temporary suspension
  • Reintroduced estimates from February 2024 should be treated with caution
  • Regional estimates have wider confidence intervals
Youth Opportunity Index

Developed by Impetus, measuring education and employment opportunities at LA level. Scores range from 0-100, with higher scores indicating better opportunities.

Definitions

NEET

Not in Education, Employment, or Training. Includes both:

  • Unemployed NEET: Seeking work and available
  • Inactive NEET: Not seeking work (reasons include disability, caring responsibilities, discouragement)
Into-Work Rate

From GBW: "The proportion of UC Searching for work regime customers who have earnings in one assessment period who did not have earnings in the preceding assessment period."

Labour Market Types

14 clusters identified using k-means clustering on 6 standardized variables. Types range from 'London Central' (high employment, young population) to 'Welsh Valleys' (high incapacity, tight labour markets).
